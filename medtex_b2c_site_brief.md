# MedTehMaks: B2C site brief

## Strategic shift

The previous B2B framing is not the main scenario.

The new site should be built primarily for B2C demand:

- private buyers
- relatives of patients
- elderly users
- people after surgery or injury
- buyers looking for home-use medical equipment
- users who want fast advice before purchase

The site must reduce anxiety, simplify choice, and convert cold Yandex Direct traffic into:

- calls
- messenger contacts
- product selection requests
- "request a quote" leads
- orders

## What works best in this market

Strong B2C medical and health-product sites usually combine:

1. Clear category-based navigation
2. Fast trust signals in the first screen
3. Assistance with selection, not just catalog browsing
4. Short path to purchase
5. Strong mobile UX
6. Real delivery, payment, warranty, and return info
7. Product pages with plain-language explanations

## Useful market patterns

### ORTEKA

Useful elements:

- phone and ordering actions visible immediately
- online consultation in the header
- services mixed with products
- catalog grouped by user logic, not only by brand
- promotions and appointment actions above the fold

### Armed

Useful elements:

- clear catalog entry
- visible discounts and new items
- search-first logic
- simple path into product groups

### Philips Healthcare / home-care pattern

Useful elements:

- trust and authority
- support and documentation visibility
- educational blocks
- stronger explanation of product purpose

## Risks on the current site

- the current domain looks temporary and weak for trust
- the site currently has only one page in sitemap
- the homepage externally triggers SmartCaptcha instead of fast access to the offer
- for Yandex Direct this creates unnecessary friction for cold traffic

## Main B2C positioning

The homepage must answer in 3 seconds:

- what exactly is sold
- for whom it is useful
- how the buyer will choose the right product
- how quickly they can get help
- why it is safe to order here

Preferred positioning model:

"Medical equipment for home use with expert help in selection, fast delivery, clear documents, and support after purchase."

## Best site model for this case

Not a single general landing page.

Best format:

- conversion-focused homepage
- category pages
- separate landing pages for ad groups
- product pages
- help / consultation pages
- delivery / payment / warranty / return pages
- reviews / trust pages

## Homepage structure

### 1. First screen

- clear B2C headline
- short explanatory subheadline
- primary CTA: "Pick the right equipment"
- secondary CTA: "Get consultation"
- visible phone
- WhatsApp / Telegram buttons
- 3-5 fast trust points

Example trust points:

- expert help with selection
- delivery across Russia
- official documents
- warranty and support
- payment options

### 2. Quick category access

Large visual category cards for the main product groups.

The user should not think where to click.

### 3. Help with selection

This block is critical for B2C.

The site should say:

- we help choose by diagnosis / task / room / age / budget
- we explain differences simply
- we do not force the buyer to understand technical specs alone

### 4. Popular products

- bestsellers
- discounted items
- new arrivals
- "often chosen" products

### 5. Why people order here

Avoid generic phrases.

Use practical reasons:

- help choosing the right model
- understandable instructions
- real delivery terms
- support after purchase
- official warranty
- documents on request

### 6. How to choose

Educational block with links such as:

- how to choose an oxygen concentrator
- what nebulizer to buy
- what recirculator fits a room
- what mattress is better for bedridden care

This improves both trust and search relevance.

### 7. Reviews and proof

- customer reviews
- ratings
- photos if available
- real brand logos
- certificates / documents

### 8. Delivery, payment, warranty

This should be visible before the footer, not hidden.

### 9. Repeated conversion block

- short form
- quick callback promise
- messenger shortcut

### 10. Contacts

- phone
- messengers
- email
- address
- working hours
- map if relevant

## Product page requirements

Each product page must include:

- product name
- clear photo
- short simple explanation: who this product is for
- key characteristics in plain language
- indications / use case
- contraindications or care notes if needed
- delivery terms
- warranty
- accessories / related products
- CTA buttons

Best CTAs:

- Ask about availability
- Help me choose
- Request price
- Buy now

## Category page requirements

Each category page should include:

- clear headline matching the search intent
- short intro in simple language
- filters
- product cards
- FAQ
- selection help CTA
- delivery / payment summary

## Yandex Direct rules for the new site

### Required approach

- separate landing pages for separate intents
- ad copy must match page headline
- the first screen must repeat the query meaning
- all key actions must work well on mobile
- no extra friction before contact

### Critical rules

- do not send all traffic to homepage
- do not use one generic page for all products
- do not hide phone or messengers
- do not force long forms
- do not put captcha in front of the first useful action

## Mobile UX requirements

- sticky header
- fixed bottom action bar on mobile
- phone + messenger + request help buttons
- large tap targets
- no horizontal scrolling
- readable fonts
- fast loading

## Tone of voice

The language should be:

- simple
- reassuring
- practical
- non-medical where possible
- non-aggressive

Avoid:

- heavy corporate tone
- abstract promises
- overtechnical text on the first screen

## Core visual direction

- clean medical-commerce style
- bright, calm, trustworthy palette
- product-first visuals
- real product imagery
- soft but structured layout
- strong typography hierarchy

Do not use:

- generic smiling stock doctors everywhere
- cluttered sliders
- overloaded menus
- too many popups
